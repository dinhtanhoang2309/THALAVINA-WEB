THE NAME BEHIND THE MISSION

The Meaning of Thalavina

A symbol of two maritime nations connecting through a shared ocean while celebrating their distinct heritage and coastal realities:

Thala The sea, inspired by thalassophile (one who loves and feels drawn to the ocean).
Vi Vietnam, representing our founder's homeland and rich coastal ecosystem.
Na Nusantara, celebrating Indonesia's archipelagic identity and ocean culture.
WHERE THE JOURNEY BEGAN

Con Dao, 2025

In 2025, founder Tan Hoang Dinh (Brandon) spent twelve nights on Hon Bay Canh in Con Dao National Park, Vietnam, assisting sea turtle conservation teams alongside IUCN Vietnam and national park rangers.

“A young person's voice does not have to be loud to be heard. Sometimes, it only has to keep rolling, like a small wave.”
— Tan Hoang Dinh (Brandon), Founder

OUR STORY & VISION

About Thalavina Project

Vision

A future where ocean protection, sustainable livelihoods, and youth leadership grow together, allowing people and nature to thrive together.

Mission

Thalavina Project aims to connect young people in Vietnam and Indonesia, and eventually wider ASEAN and Asia-Pacific communities, with accessible ocean knowledge and opportunities for meaningful action.

Six Guiding Principles

  • Ocean stewardship: Caring for marine habitats as interconnected living ecosystems.
  • Knowledge for action: Transforming complex science into tangible community tools.
  • Youth leadership: Centering emerging youth voices in environmental dialogue.
  • Cross-border collaboration: Uniting Vietnam, Indonesia, and ASEAN across shared waters.
  • Environmental & social responsibility: Grounding ecological work in community justice.
  • Evidence and integrity: Upholding verified facts, transparency, and scientific honesty.
ADVOCACY & BACKGROUND

Tan Hoang Dinh (Brandon)

Founder • Vietnamese • Development Economics

Tan Hoang Dinh, widely known as Brandon, is a Vietnamese youth advocate, a Development Economics student, and the founder of Thalavina Project.

His advocacy journey began during the COVID-19 pandemic, when he continued participating in youth forums, volunteering, and community activities in Vietnam. Over time, he became increasingly interested in SDG 14 (Life Below Water) and the interconnections between ocean climate, biodiversity, coastal livelihoods, and community resilience.

A formative experience came during twelve nights at Hon Bay Canh, Con Dao National Park in 2025, gaining practical sea turtle conservation experience alongside IUCN Vietnam and the national park team.

PILLAR 01

Ocean Climate Protection

Covering ocean warming, marine heatwaves, acidification, sea-level rise, coastal resilience, and marine ecosystems.

The ocean absorbs over 90% of excess heat in the climate system. Our focus is translating global ocean climate findings into localized knowledge for Southeast Asian coastal communities.

PILLAR 02

Sustainable Blue Economy

Examines how economic opportunity, healthy marine ecosystems, and sustainable coastal livelihoods can develop together.

We approach marine resources from an economic and social perspective, addressing just transition, sustainable fishery stewardship, and equitable distribution of coastal resources.

PILLAR 03

Sea Turtle Conservation

Learning about sea turtle lifecycles, nesting beaches, hatchling survival, and community-led conservation efforts.

Inspired by practical field volunteering at Hon Bay Canh in Con Dao National Park in 2025, we emphasize public education and ethical wildlife stewardship.
